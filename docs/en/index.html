Where would you keep 1,000 coins?

The same 1,000 coins sit in a wallet, a savings account and three funds. Skip ahead one week at a time and watch what happens to each. It all follows the same rules as the app.

Does "save it and it grows" ever really land with a kid?

Interest, compounding and inflation sound further away the more you explain them. In SukSuk Allowance, kids earn it, spend it and wait for it themselves. Once they spot interest sitting on last week's savings, the next allowance goes straight into the savings account.

Watch patience pay off

Money in the savings account earns compound interest every week. You set the rate, and a chart shows what it will grow to in 12 weeks if they leave it alone.
Missed a few days? When the app opens, every week it owes gets paid at once.

See prices eat into a wallet

The Market tab puts your family's price index, inflation rate, interest rate, money supply and fund prices on one screen. As prices climb, it shows how much less 1,000 coins in the wallet will now buy.
Kids see in plain numbers that money left idle shrinks, and money saved or invested holds up.

Questions

Is any real money involved?

No. The currency and every transaction in the app are pretend, for learning only. Nothing connects to a real bank account, card or cash.

How do we see the same ledger on my child's phone?

Devices signed in to the same iCloud account keep the family ledger in sync automatically. Check that iCloud is on in Settings > Apple Account > iCloud. Under Invite Child on the Actions tab, you can make a QR invitation for your child's camera to scan. Without iCloud, data stays on that one device.

What is Child Phone Mode?

It turns your child's phone into a screen made just for them. They only see their own wallet, investments, history and lessons, and leaving it takes the parent PIN.

I forgot the parent PIN.

The PIN is stored only on the device, so not even the developer can recover it. Deleting and reinstalling the app lets you start setup again, but this also erases your existing data.

How does savings interest work?

Compound interest is added to the savings balance every week, automatically. You set the rate in Parent Mode, and any weeks that passed are settled the next time the app opens.

Are fund prices tied to the real market?

No. Fund prices are practice data that change once a day inside the app. The riskier the fund, the bigger the swings. They are not real financial products.

Why do prices go up?

So kids can feel inflation, your family price index rises a little each week. Change the rate, or set it to 0% to turn it off, in Parent Mode under Bank Settings. Nudging up the prices of snacks and toys in the Family Shop to match makes the lesson stick.

How do I delete everything?

Deleting the app removes the data stored on the device. For data synced to iCloud, go to Settings > Apple Account > iCloud > Manage Storage and delete the app's data.

Is my family's information sent anywhere?

No. There's no sign-up, and all data stays on your device and in your own iCloud account. The developer only receives feedback you send yourself from Parent Mode, plus anonymous usage stats and crash reports.

Which devices does it run on?

iPhone with iOS 17.0 or later.
