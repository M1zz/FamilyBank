🐣 What is SukSuk Allowance?

SukSuk Allowance is an iOS app that teaches kids about money using a currency that only exists inside your family. Parents act as the central bank and issue the allowance, while kids make purchases, earn interest on their savings and invest in pretend funds, learning how the economy works by doing it themselves.

All your data stays on your device and in your own iCloud account. There is no sign-up, and nothing your family enters is ever sent to the developer.

✨ Key Features

  • Your own family currency — Name it yourself: Beans, Stars, Coins, anything you like
  • Pay and deduct allowance — Managed in Parent Mode, protected by a PIN
  • Purchases — Kids pay for snacks, game time and more on their own, with barcode payments
  • Savings interest — Compound interest is added to the savings account automatically every week
  • Investing practice — Steady 🐢 · Balanced 🐰 · Bold 🚀 pretend funds
  • Transaction history — Look back at where every coin went, day by day
  • Money lessons — Simple guides to currency, saving, compound interest, investing, opportunity cost and more
